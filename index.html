About me

[cite_start]I am a Ph.D. student in Biostatistics at the University of Florida (Aug 2022 - Dec 2026)[cite: 14, 15, 20]. [cite_start]My research as a Doctorate Research Assistant at the Sepsis and Critical Illness Research Center [cite: 23, 24] [cite_start]focuses on driving translational research by applying multi-omics data to uncover immune mechanisms in critical illness[cite: 26]. I design and validate robust, reproducible end-to-end bioinformatics pipelines for sequencing data (e.g., scRNA-seq, bulk [cite_start]RNA-seq) using R/Python in an HPC environment[cite: 27].

[cite_start]Previously, I worked as a Health Data Specialist at the New Jersey Department of Health[cite: 29, 31], where I applied [cite_start]real-world data analytics and generalized linear modeling (GLMs) to evaluate Hepatitis C burden[cite: 33]. [cite_start]I received my M.S. in Biostatistics from Rutgers University [cite: 16] (2018-2020) [cite_start][cite: 21] and my B.S. in Biology [cite_start]from Peking University [cite: 17, 18] (2014-2018) [cite_start][cite: 22].
